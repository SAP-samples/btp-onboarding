# Cash and Treasury Assistant - DC Mission

This directory contains the files for the **Cash and Treasury Assistant DC Mission** — a structured guide for activating, configuring, and validating the Cash and Treasury Assistant within SAP Autonomous Finance.

## Overview

The Cash and Treasury Assistant supports cash managers and corporate treasurers in managing daily cash positions and bank relationships. With a simple command in natural language, you can check daily cash positions, identify surplus and deficit accounts, initiate bank transfers, and analyse bank relationships across accounts, credit lines, investments, and bank fees. All proposed actions require human approval before execution.

> This guide targets SAP administrators and finance teams activating the Cash and Treasury Assistant for their organisation.
