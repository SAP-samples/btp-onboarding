## Cash and Treasury Orchestration Agent

### Objective

Supports cash managers and corporate treasurers with their daily tasks through natural language interactions. The agent identifies user intent and coordinates the appropriate underlying agents — Cash Positioning Agent and Bank Relationship Agent — to provide real-time insights and assistance.

### Prerequisites

- Cash and Treasury Assistant activated in SAP for Me
- At least one sub-agent (Cash Positioning Agent or Bank Relationship Agent) configured and authorised

> Always start your conversation with **@Cash and Treasury Assistant** to ensure the correct context is established. Type `@c...` in the chat and select it from the list.

### What It Can Do

- Route natural language cash and treasury requests to the correct specialised agent
- Orchestrate bank relationship analysis and cash positioning tasks
- Provide real-time insights by coordinating data from multiple sources
- Support cash managers and treasurers with daily treasury operations

### Example Prompts

| Prompt |
|--------|
| "@Cash and Treasury Assistant Perform daily cash positioning." |
| "@Cash and Treasury Assistant What is our relationship with Bank ABC?" |
