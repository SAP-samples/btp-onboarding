## Cash and Treasury Assistant — Solution Overview

The **Cash and Treasury Assistant** is a Joule Assistant within SAP Autonomous Finance. It runs on SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition and supports cash managers and corporate treasurers in managing daily cash positions and bank relationships.

With a simple command in natural language, you can check daily cash positions, identify surplus and deficit accounts, initiate bank transfers, and analyse bank relationships across accounts, credit lines, investments, and bank fees. The assistant helps you make informed decisions on fund allocation and banking strategy, turning data from multiple sources into clear, actionable insights.

> To interact with the Cash and Treasury Assistant, always start your conversation with **@Cash and Treasury Assistant**. Type `@c...` in the Joule chat and select it from the list of assistants.

### Agents Used by This Assistant

| Agent | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Cash and Treasury Orchestration Agent | Routes natural language cash and treasury requests to the appropriate sub-agent based on intent |
| Cash Positioning Agent | Manages daily cash positioning tasks — checks opening balances, forecasts cash flows, calculates closing balances, and proposes bank transfers |
| Bank Relationship Agent | Analyses bank relationships from multiple perspectives — balances, payment volumes, credit lines, borrowings, investments, and bank fees |

### Setup Process at a Glance

1. **Prepare** — Confirm that all prerequisites are in place before activation
2. **Set Up** — Provision the Cash and Treasury Assistant in SAP for Me and assign users in SAP Cloud Identity Services
3. **Agents** — Explore the three specialised agents, their capabilities, and example prompts
