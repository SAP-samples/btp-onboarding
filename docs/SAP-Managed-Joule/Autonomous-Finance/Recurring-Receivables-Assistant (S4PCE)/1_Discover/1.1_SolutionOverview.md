## Recurring Receivables Assistant — Solution Overview

The **Recurring Receivables Assistant** is a Joule Assistant within SAP Autonomous Finance. It runs on SAP S/4HANA Cloud, Private Edition (RISE with SAP) and automates order-to-cash processes for recurring billing, subscription-based revenue, and contract accounting.

It coordinates specialised agents to manage collection insights, invoice corrections, and subscription contract lifecycle changes. All corrective actions require human approval before execution.

### Prerequisites Summary

- SAP S/4HANA Cloud, Private Edition release 2025 FPS01 or later
- Contract Accounting (FI-CA) module active and configured

### Agents Used by This Assistant

| Agent | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| Collection Insights Agent | Delivers intelligent account insights based on contract account balance analysis, payment behaviour, and past collection activities |
| Convergent Invoice Execution Agent | Automates invoice corrections including rebilling and invoice reprinting |
| Subscription Lifecycle Agent | Retrieves, validates, simulates, and executes subscription contract changes |

### Setup Process at a Glance

1. **Prepare** — Confirm that all prerequisites are in place before activation
2. **Set Up** — Provision the Recurring Receivables Assistant in SAP for Me and assign users in SAP Cloud Identity Services
3. **Agents** — Explore the three specialised agents, their capabilities, and example prompts
4. **Complete** — Share your feedback and mark the mission as done
