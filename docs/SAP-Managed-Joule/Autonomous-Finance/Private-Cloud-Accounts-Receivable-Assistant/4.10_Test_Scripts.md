## Test Scripts — Accounts Receivable Assistant (Private Cloud)

> Source: *Accounts Receivable Assistant UAT Script, Sep 2026 (Private Cloud)* — Version 1.0
> Audience: AR Accountants and Collection Specialists, Testers

---

## Purpose

This script guides you through end-to-end testing of the AR Assistant. The AR Assistant connects the following specialized agents:

- **Receivable Account Analysis Agent** — Provides AR Accountants with a comprehensive overview of customer accounts for a company code, offering insights into customer balances, line items, invoices, aging, disputes, and dunning processes.
- **Dunning Insights Agent** — Analyzes dunning history to offer insights and recommendations. Assists in timely follow-ups, suggests setting or removing dunning blocks.
- **Collections Account Preparation Agent** — Provides collection specialists with a 360-degree view of customer account for a collection segment including contact history, financial summaries, aging analysis, promise-to-pay summaries, and dispute summary.
- **Collections Email Outreach Agent** — Creates customer contact and sends correspondence emails to customer contacts for selected worklist items, including overdue invoices and broken or upcoming promises to pay.
- **Dispute Creation Agent** — Enables creation of dispute cases by processing unstructured data like user prompts or email content using natural language processing. Validates existence of billing documents and duplicate cases.
- **Dispute Resolution Agent** — Automates validation of dispute cases and suggests actionable solutions. Reviews invoices, sales orders, delivery records to ascertain validity of dispute claims and offers tailored recommendations.

---

## What You Are Testing

- The agents are reachable from Joule and respond without errors
- Each agent produces output that is accurate, complete, and relevant to the event
- The human-in-the-loop steps (confirming dispute creation, setting or removing dunning blocks, etc.) work as expected
- Actions taken in Joule are correctly reflected in the connected S/4HANA Cloud system

## Prerequisites

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| S/4HANA Cloud access | Log in to SAP S/4HANA Cloud with roles `SAP_BR_AR_ACCOUNTANT` and `SAP_BR_AR_MANAGER` assigned to your user |
| Joule enabled | The Diamond (Joule) icon is visible in the top right corner of the SAP S/4HANA landing page |
| SAP FSCM | SAP FSCM Collections Management and SAP FSCM Dispute Management must be active |
| Master data | Customer accounts, customizations for Collections, Dispute Management, and Email must be configured in the S/4 system |
| Policy documents | Policy documents for Dispute Resolution Agent and Collections Email Outreach Agent must be uploaded to Joule before first use |

---

## How to Use This Script

- Work through each Part in order. Follow the test script by executing the prompts.
- Record your result in the Pass / Fail column for every test case.
- Use the Notes column to capture any unexpected behavior, error messages, or UI issues — even if the test passes overall.
- If a test case fails, note what happened and continue to the next case unless the failure prevents further steps in that section.
- Edge Cases (Part C) can be run at the end of the session.

**Defects:** Log in Jira and include the TC number, the exact message typed into Joule, and a screenshot of the result.

### Receivables and Payables Clearing Agent — AR Side

> Source: `2026_E2E_APAR_AGENTS_AR.docx`
> Role: `SAP_BR_AR_ACCOUNTANT`; App: **Manage Customer Clearing Proposals** (F9287)

| # | Scenario | Steps | Expected Result |
|---|----------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Invoke agent | Open Joule and invoke `@Accounts Receivable Assistant`; request clearing of customer open items for a company code and customer | Agent acknowledges and starts processing |

| 4 | Review reasoning | Click **Reasoning** icon on a proposal | Agent explains why open items were grouped together |
| 5 | Post proposal | Select a proposal and click **Post** (HITL) | Open items move from "Open" tab to "All" tab with status "Cleared Item" |
| 6 | Delete proposal | Select and delete an unwanted proposal or entire set | Proposal removed from list |
