## Post-Provisioning Validations (PCE)

After provisioning, validate that the CIS application and groups are correctly configured, and verify agent connectivity.

### CIS Application and Groups

In your SAP Cloud Identity Services (CIS) admin console:

1. Navigate to **Applications** and confirm the Joule application is listed.
2. Navigate to **Users & Authorizations → Groups** and confirm the following group exists:
   - `joule_end_user`

> If the groups are missing, they are created automatically during provisioning. Allow a few minutes and refresh.

### AMS Policy Assignment

AMS policies must be assigned to users in SAP Cloud Identity Services (IDP).

**Prerequisites:** You have admin access to the SAP Cloud Identity Service tenant, and rights to manage applications and user assignments.

**Steps:**

1. Sign in to the IDP tenant.
2. Navigate to **Users & Authorizations**.
3. Search for groups with the display name **"agent – Access"**.
4. Pick the respective entry for the agent from the search results.
5. Assign users to the authorization group by clicking the **+ Add** button.

### Agent-Specific Validations

After provisioning, verify the following for the Cash and Treasury Assistant:

#### Required Roles

Confirm the following business roles are assigned to the user before testing:

| Role | Required For |
|------|-------------|
| Business role with treasury authorisations (TRM or Cash Management) | All Cash and Treasury agents (common prerequisite) |

#### Functional Checks

- Log in to Joule and confirm the Cash and Treasury Assistant agents are accessible.
- Test the Cash Positioning Agent by requesting the current cash position for a known bank account or company code.
- Test the Bank Relationship Agent by requesting bank account information for a known bank.
- Confirm that the treasury user has the required authorisations to access cash management and bank account data.
