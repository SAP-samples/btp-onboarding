## Provision Cash and Treasury Assistant in SAP for Me (Public Cloud)

Once all pre-requisites are in place, the Cash and Treasury Assistant is activated through SAP for Me.

**Step 1 – Log in to SAP for Me**

Go to [https://me.sap.com](https://me.sap.com) and sign in with your S-user ID.

**Step 2 – Navigate to the Autonomous Enterprise**

From the sidebar, select **Portfolio & Products**, then open **My Product Packages** and select the catalog **Autonomous Enterprise**. This lists all available Autonomous Enterprise areas — select **Autonomous Finance**.
<img width="800" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/17ac5f74-0146-47f9-a416-8afad24142ec" />

**Step 3 – Locate the Cash and Treasury Assistant**

In the feature list, find **Cash and Treasury Assistant** under Autonomous Finance.
<img width="800" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/89324a31-6935-402e-a3fb-d9efbf2fb954" />

**Step 4 – Start Provisioning**

Select the feature and click **Start Provisioning**. SAP for Me will guide you through the activation wizard. During provisioning, you will be prompted to select which sub-agents to activate. Activate the agents relevant to your deployment.
