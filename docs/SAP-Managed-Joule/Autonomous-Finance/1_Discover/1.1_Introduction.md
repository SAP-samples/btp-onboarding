## SAP Autonomous Finance — Introduction

**SAP Autonomous Finance** is a suite of AI-powered assistants built on Joule that automate finance and sales operations processes within SAP S/4HANA Cloud. Each assistant targets a specific business process — such as resolving sales order fulfilment issues, managing billing blocks, or handling credit situations — and enables teams to act faster with less manual effort.

All assistants operate with a human-in-the-loop model: the AI identifies issues, analyses the situation, and recommends or proposes corrective actions, but execution always requires human approval.

### Available Assistants

| Assistant | Business Process |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Sales Operation Assistant | Resolution of sales order fulfilment issues — delivery blocks, billing blocks, credit blocks, and supply problems |
| Financial Closing Assistant | Automation of period-end financial closing activities |
| Accounts Payable Assistant | Monitoring and resolution of accounts payable issues |
| Accounts Receivable Assistant | Monitoring and resolution of accounts receivable issues |
| Recurring Receivables Assistant | Management of recurring billing and receivables |
| Cash and Treasury Assistant | Cash position monitoring and treasury operations |

### Key Principles

- **AI-assisted, human-approved** — Assistants recommend actions; humans decide and execute
- **Embedded in the workflow** — Available directly through Joule in SAP Build Work Zone
- **Context-aware** — Each assistant has access to the relevant SAP S/4HANA data and documents needed to analyse the situation
