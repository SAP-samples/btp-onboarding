## Post-Provisioning Validations (PCE)

After provisioning, validate that the CIS application and groups are correctly configured, and verify agent connectivity.

### CIS Application and Groups

In your SAP Cloud Identity Services (CIS) admin console:

1. Navigate to **Applications** and confirm the Joule application is listed.
2. Navigate to **Users & Authorizations → Groups** and confirm the following groups exist:
   - `joule_end_user`
   - `joule_tenant_admin` (for administrators)

> If the groups are missing, they are created automatically during provisioning. Allow a few minutes and refresh.

### Agent-Specific Validations

After provisioning, verify the following for the Sales Operations Assistant:

#### Required Roles

Confirm the following business roles are assigned to the user before testing:

| Role | Required For |
|------|-------------|
| `SAP_BR_INTERNAL_SALES_REP` | All Sales Operations agents (common prerequisite) |
| `SAP_BR_BILLING_CLERK` | Billing Block Resolution Agent (additional role required) |

#### Functional Checks

- Log in to Joule and confirm the Sales Operations Assistant agents are accessible.
- Test the Credit Block Resolution Agent by requesting a list of sales orders blocked due to credit limits.
- Test the Billing Block Resolution Agent by requesting a list of sales orders blocked for billing.
- Test the Delivery Issue Resolution Agent by requesting details on sales orders blocked for delivery.
- Test the Supply Issue Resolution Agent by requesting analysis of supply-related issues for a known material or order.
- Confirm the required OData services (`API_SALESDOCUMENTS_AI`, `SD_SOF`, `API_SALES_ORDER_SRV`) are active in your system.
