## Provision Recurring Receivables Assistant in SAP for Me (PCE)

> **Note:** This assistant is currently part of an SAP Early Adopter Care (EAC) program. Access must be requested through your SAP Account Executive before proceeding with provisioning.

Once all pre-requisites are in place and EAC access has been granted, the Recurring Receivables Assistant is activated through SAP for Me.

**Step 1 – Log in to SAP for Me**

Go to [https://me.sap.com](https://me.sap.com) and sign in with your S-user ID.

**Step 2 – Navigate to the Autonomous Enterprise**

From the sidebar, select **Portfolio & Products**, then open **My Product Packages** and select the catalog **Autonomous Enterprise**. This lists all available Autonomous Enterprise areas — select **Autonomous Finance**.

**Step 3 – Locate the Recurring Receivables Assistant**

In the feature list, find **Recurring Receivables Assistant** under Autonomous Finance.

**Step 4 – Start Provisioning**

Select the feature and click **Start Provisioning**. SAP for Me will guide you through the activation wizard. During provisioning, you will be prompted to select which agents to activate. Activate the agents relevant to your use case.

> **Note:** The Collection Insights Agent requires Contract Accounting (FI-CA) as part of your SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition. Ensure FI-CA is activated before enabling this agent.

**Step 5 – Confirm Activation**

Once SAP processes the request, the Recurring Receivables Assistant becomes available in your Joule interface on SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition.
