## Support Contact (PCE)

For issues related to the Recurring Receivables Assistant on SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition, use the following support channels.

### SAP Support

- Open a support incident via [SAP for Me](https://me.sap.com) under the relevant component.
- Recommended component: **CA-ML-AFA** (Autonomous Finance Assistants)

### Useful Resources

- [SAP Help — Recurring Receivables Assistant](https://help.sap.com/docs/joule/autonomous-finance/recurring-receivables-assistant)

> **Note:** As this assistant is part of the SAP Early Adopter Care (EAC) program, your SAP Account Executive is the primary escalation path for access and feature-related questions. Additional support contacts will be added here once the assistant becomes generally available.
