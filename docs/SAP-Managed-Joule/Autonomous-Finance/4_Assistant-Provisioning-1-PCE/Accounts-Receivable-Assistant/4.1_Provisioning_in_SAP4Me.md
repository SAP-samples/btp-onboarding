## Provision Accounts Receivable Assistant in SAP for Me (PCE)

Once all pre-requisites are in place, the Accounts Receivable Assistant is activated through SAP for Me.

**Step 1 – Log in to SAP for Me**

Go to [https://me.sap.com](https://me.sap.com) and sign in with your S-user ID.

**Step 2 – Navigate to the Autonomous Enterprise**

From the sidebar, select **Portfolio & Products**, then open **My Product Packages** and select the catalog **Autonomous Enterprise**. This lists all available Autonomous Enterprise areas — select **Autonomous Finance**.

**Step 3 – Locate the Accounts Receivable Assistant**

In the feature list, find **Accounts Receivable Assistant** under Autonomous Finance.

**Step 4 – Start Provisioning**

Select the feature and click **Start Provisioning**. SAP for Me will guide you through the activation wizard. During provisioning, you will be prompted to select which sub-agents to activate. Activate the agents relevant to your use case.

> **Note:** To use the `@mention` feature (typing `@Accounts Receivable Assistant` in Joule) and ensure specialized agents are orchestrated correctly, use the Accounts Receivable Orchestration Agent as your entry point rather than invoking sub-agents directly.

**Step 5 – Confirm Activation**

Once SAP processes the request, the Accounts Receivable Assistant becomes available in your Joule interface on SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition.
