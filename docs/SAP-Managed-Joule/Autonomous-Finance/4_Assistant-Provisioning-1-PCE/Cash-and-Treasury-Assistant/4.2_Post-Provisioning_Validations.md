## Post-Provisioning Validations (PCE)

After provisioning, validate that the required BTP resources, formation, CIS application, and groups are correctly configured.

### Resources

Verify that the following BTP resources are active in your subaccount:

- SAP Joule service instance
- SAP Build Work Zone service instance (if applicable)

### Formation

Confirm that the **Autonomous Finance** formation is visible and active in your BTP subaccount under **Services → Instances and Subscriptions**.

### CIS Application and Groups

In your SAP Cloud Identity Services (CIS) admin console:

1. Navigate to **Applications** and confirm the Joule application is listed.
2. Navigate to **Users & Authorizations → Groups** and confirm the following groups exist:
   - `joule_end_user`
   - `joule_tenant_admin` (for administrators)

> If the groups are missing, they are created automatically during provisioning. Allow a few minutes and refresh.

### Agent-Specific Validations

After provisioning, verify the following for the Cash and Treasury Assistant:

- Log in to Joule and confirm the Cash and Treasury Assistant agents are accessible.
- Test the Cash Positioning Agent by requesting the current cash position for a known bank account or company code.
- Test the Bank Relationship Agent by requesting bank account information for a known bank.
- Confirm that the treasury user has the required authorisations to access cash management and bank account data.
