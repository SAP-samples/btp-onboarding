## Post-Provisioning Validations (Public Cloud)

After provisioning, validate that the CIS application and groups are correctly configured, and verify agent connectivity.

### CIS Application and Groups

In your SAP Cloud Identity Services (CIS) admin console:

1. Navigate to **Applications** and confirm the Joule application is listed.
2. Navigate to **Users & Authorizations → Groups** and confirm the following group exists:
   - `joule_end_user`

> If the groups are missing, they are created automatically during provisioning. Allow a few minutes and refresh.

### Agent-Specific Validations

After provisioning, verify the following for the International Trade Assistant:

#### Required Roles

Confirm the following business roles are assigned to the user before testing:

| Role | Required For |
|------|-------------|
| Trade compliance specialist / international trade user role | All International Trade agents (common prerequisite) |

#### Functional Checks

- Log in to Joule and confirm the International Trade Assistant agents are accessible.
- Test the International Trade Classification Agent by requesting goods classification for a known material or product.
- Test the International Trade Product Profile Agent by requesting a product profile for a known material.
- Confirm that users have the required trade compliance authorizations before testing agents.
