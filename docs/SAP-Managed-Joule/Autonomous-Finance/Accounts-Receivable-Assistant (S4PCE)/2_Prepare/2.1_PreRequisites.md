## Pre-Requisites

The following components must be in place before the Accounts Receivable Assistant can be activated.

- **SAP S/4HANA Cloud, Private Edition** — Release 2025 FPS01 or later required.

- **SAP Managed Joule (SMJ) — fully provisioned** — Joule via SAP-Managed Provisioning, version 2025 FPS01 or later. For provisioning instructions, refer to the [SAP Managed Joule Provisioning guide](https://help.sap.com/docs/joule/joule-work-621104a90a01407fbb2d1613db39a54d/sap-managed-provisioning).

- **Business Role** — Business role `SAP_BR_AR_ACCOUNTANT` assigned to all users requiring access.

- **FI Components** — SAP Dispute Management (FIN-FSCM-DM) and Collections Management (FIN-FSCM-COL) must be active.

- **SAP Cloud Identity Services (CIS) — active tenant** — Users must be assigned to the `joule_end_user` group in CIS (see section 3.2).

- **S-user with SAP for Me authorisations** — The activating user must have **Display Cloud Data** and **Edit Cloud Data** permissions in SAP for Me.
