# Accounts Receivable Assistant - DC Mission

This directory contains the files for the **Accounts Receivable Assistant DC Mission** — a structured guide for activating, configuring, and validating the Accounts Receivable Assistant within SAP Autonomous Finance.

## Overview

The Accounts Receivable Assistant helps finance teams accelerate cash collection, resolve disputes efficiently, and manage credit risk proactively. It improves operational efficiency and accelerates cash realisation by reducing manual activities, minimising delays, and ensuring consistent and compliant financial execution. The Accounts Receivable Orchestration Agent coordinates six specialised agents covering collection preparation, dispute management, clearing, and dunning. All corrective actions require human approval before execution.

> This guide targets SAP administrators and finance teams activating the Accounts Receivable Assistant for their organisation.
